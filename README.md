# Skylark

Skylark is a block-based WikiHouse timber building system designed for 1–3 storey construction.

For more information, product guides, support services, and terms & conditions, visit:

- https://www.wikihouse.cc
- https://www.wikihouse.cc/terms

## Notes on this release

Version v1.0 supersedes the previous release. The following changes have been made:

1. **Internal ribs removed from walls**  
   Internal ribs were removed as, although they provide space for services, they significantly complicate and slow down the installation of the vapour barrier on site. Their removal also simplifies assembly and makes insulation of wall blocks easier.

2. **Removable top panel on beams**  
   The top panel of beams is now removable to allow easy access to the internal cavity. It can be fixed in place with brad nails after insulation is installed.

3. **Removable pocket at floor–column joint**  
   A removable pocket panel has been introduced to facilitate installation of vertical ties at the floor–column connection.

4. **Wider end floor blocks**  
   The previous thinner end floor blocks were difficult to align during assembly. These have been merged with standard floor blocks to create a wider, more user-friendly element.

5. **Wider corner blocks**  
   The previous smaller corner blocks allowed greater design flexibility but contributed less to lateral stiffness. The new wider blocks include additional hold-down connections, improving overall structural stability and strength. Corner blocks can also be rotated and used in either direction, increasing flexibility during design and assembly.
